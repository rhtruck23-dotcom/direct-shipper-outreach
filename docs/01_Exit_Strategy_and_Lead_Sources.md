# Exit Strategy: From Broker-Dependent Carrier to Direct-Shipper Fleet

## Reality check
- Direct accounts take 30–90 days. Keep boards/brokers for cash flow while this pipeline builds.
- Target 3–5 direct accounts first. One steady reefer account at 2–3 loads/week beats 20 broker loads.
- Reefer helps you — produce, meat, dairy, frozen distributors need reliable capacity.

## Who to approach directly
- Produce packers & shippers
- Cold storage & 3PL warehouses (the warehouse operator, not their broker arm)
- Food & beverage distributors, dairies, meat processors
- Manufacturers (dry van), grocery DCs, building-material / retail DCs (box)

## Where to get real leads (this app does not invent emails)
1. State Department of Agriculture directories
2. USDA Market News / AMS directories
3. ThomasNet.com — search, then **export / copy** into CSV Import (do not scrape)
4. Google Places search inside this app (legal API) + add logistics email from their site/phone
5. Trade association member directories
6. LinkedIn — find the person, then **manual or Sales Navigator export** into CSV
7. Paid databases (Data Axle, ZoomInfo) once serious
8. Cold call the front desk: “Who handles inbound freight?”

## Legal
- CAN-SPAM: real company name, physical address, honest subjects, honor STOP
- No LinkedIn/ThomasNet automated scraping in this tool
